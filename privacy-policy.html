Privacy Policy

Putting Improver by Lock Jaw Disc Golf
Last Updated: February 4, 2026

Summary: We collect minimal data needed to provide the app's features. We don't sell your data, and you can delete your account and data at any time.

1. Introduction

Lock Jaw Disc Golf LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Putting Improver mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

By using Putting Improver, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and profile picture (via Google Sign-In)
  • Profile Data: Optional information like favorite disc, birthday, and location preferences
  • Practice Data: Putting sessions, routines, games, scores, and statistics you log
  • Social Data: Friend connections, challenges, and leaderboard participation

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: App features used, session frequency, and interaction patterns
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Analytics: Aggregated, anonymized data about app performance

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise GPS location (only general timezone if needed)
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos or camera data (except profile pictures you upload)
  • Financial information (payments handled by app stores)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Track your putting practice progress and statistics
  • Enable social features like leaderboards and friend challenges
  • Send important updates about the Service
  • Improve and optimize the app experience
  • Respond to your support requests

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Public Information

The following information may be visible to other users:

  • Display name and profile picture
  • Leaderboard rankings and scores
  • Achievement badges earned
  • Challenge results (with participants only)

4.2 Service Providers

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, database, and hosting
  • Google Analytics: Anonymized usage statistics

4.3 We Do NOT

  • Sell your personal data to third parties
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your data for targeted advertising

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure, which employs industry-standard encryption and security practices. Data is stored in the United States.

While we implement reasonable security measures, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong password and protect your login credentials.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data we have about you in your profile settings
  • Correct: Update your profile information at any time
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export: Request a copy of your data
  • Opt-out: Hide your profile from public leaderboards

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@puttingimprover.com or use the in-app settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Profile and practice data: Deleted within 30 days
  • Aggregated/anonymized statistics: May be retained indefinitely
  • Backup systems: Purged within 90 days

8. Children's Privacy

Putting Improver is intended for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

9. International Users

If you are accessing the Service from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from your country.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by:

  • Posting the new policy in the app
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an email for material changes

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights
