🥏 PUTTING IMPROVER - Your Personal Disc Golf Putting Coach

Transform your putting game with structured practice sessions, competitive games, and detailed analytics. Whether you're a beginner learning the basics or a tournament player fine-tuning your accuracy, Putting Improver helps you track progress and stay motivated.

📊 TRACK YOUR PRACTICE
• Log putting sessions from any distance (5-70+ feet)
• Record makes, attempts, and accuracy percentages
• Track weather conditions and session notes
• View detailed statistics and progress charts
• Monitor your longest practice streaks

🎮 10 INTERACTIVE GAMES
• HORSE - Challenge friends to match shots
• Around the World - Hit stations in sequence
• Distance Ladder - Climb from close to far
• Putting Par - Beat par on 9 putting holes
• Points Poker - Strategic risk/reward scoring
• Putt 100 - Classic 100-putt challenge
• Perfect 10 - Consecutive makes challenge
• Joe's Monday Night - Tournament-style pressure
• 21 - First to exactly 21 points
• Tic-Tac-Throw - Strategic grid putting

📋 20+ PRACTICE ROUTINES
• Beginner: Build confidence from close range
• Intermediate: Mix distances and add pressure
• Advanced: Long-range and tournament prep
• Expert: Ultimate 100-putt challenges
• Create custom routines for your goals

🏆 ACHIEVEMENTS & PROGRESSION
• 190+ achievements to unlock
• Daily and weekly challenges
• Season XP system with rewards
• Level up and earn badges
• Track your all-time records

📈 COMPETE & COMPARE
• Global leaderboards (Points, ELO, Season)
• Head-to-head challenges with friends
• Friend system with activity feeds
• Compare stats and progress

✨ ADDITIONAL FEATURES
• Works offline - sync when connected
• Multiplayer logging for groups
• Dark mode desert theme
• No ads in core experience
• Regular updates with new content

PERFECT FOR:
✓ Beginners building fundamentals
✓ Casual players tracking improvement
✓ Competitive players preparing for tournaments
✓ Coaches tracking student progress
✓ Friends competing together
