Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Putting Improver collects the following information when you use our service:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Practice Data: We store your practice sessions, game scores, routine completions, and performance statistics.
  • Usage Data: We may collect information about how you use the app, including features accessed and time spent.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Putting Improver service
  • Track your putting practice progress and statistics
  • Display leaderboards and enable social features
  • Award achievements and calculate points
  • Improve and personalize your experience
  • Communicate with you about the service

3. Information Sharing

Your practice data is used for the following public features:

  • Leaderboards: Your name, total points, and ranking may be visible to other users
  • Community Content: Custom routines and games you mark as "public" are visible to all users
  • Player Search: Other users can search for you and view your public statistics

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase:

  • All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access to your data is protected by authentication
  • We implement industry-standard security measures
  • Data is stored on secure servers maintained by Google

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all your personal data stored in the app
  • Edit: Modify or update your practice sessions, games, and routines
  • Delete: Remove individual sessions or your entire account
  • Control Sharing: Mark custom content as private to prevent sharing

6. Authentication

We use Google Sign-In for authentication. By signing in, you agree to:

  • Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
  • Allow us to access your basic Google profile information

7. Children's Privacy

Putting Improver is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use the following:

  • Authentication Cookies: To keep you signed in
  • Local Storage: To cache app data for better performance

9. Third-Party Services

Putting Improver uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: For authentication, database, and hosting
  • Google Fonts: For typography

These services have their own privacy policies governing their data collection.

10. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion at any time by contacting us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
